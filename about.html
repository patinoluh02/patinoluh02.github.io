About Me

Physics, materials science, and computational modeling.

I am an undergraduate researcher studying physics and geography with a particular interest in materials science and the relationship between physical mechanisms, computational models, and experiments.

Research Interests

I am particularly interested in research that combines computational methods with experimental investigation. Computational tools can reveal mechanisms that are difficult to directly observe, while experiments provide the physical constraints needed to test and refine those models.

My experience has included semiconductor nanomaterials, photocatalysis, spin-wave propagation, rheology, multiphysics simulation, scientific computing, and experimental characterization.

Research Approach

I am interested in approaching materials problems by moving between theory, simulation, synthesis, characterization, and experiment. Rather than treating computation and experiment as separate areas, I see them as complementary ways of understanding the same physical system.

Current Goals

I plan to pursue graduate study in materials science and engineering, with an emphasis on computational and experimental approaches to understanding and developing functional materials.
